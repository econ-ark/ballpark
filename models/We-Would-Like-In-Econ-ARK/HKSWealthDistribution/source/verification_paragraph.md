I accepted Matsya’s overall two-stage decomposition of the HKS household problem into a consumption stage and a shock stage, because this matches the timing of the Bellman problem in the paper. I also accepted the distinction between individual states, the single control, idiosyncratic shocks, and aggregate prices/policy objects, and I revised the markdown to add calibration and process details drawn from the paper. I kept the YAML files explicitly as drafts, because several representation issues remain unresolved, especially the treatment of the stochastic discount factor as a state, the function valued objects tau(.), r_X(.), sigma_X(.), and psi(.), and the inter-stage/inter-period wiring. I therefore treat the YAML as a structured model draft that is faithful to the paper’s household problem, but not yet as a final executable dolo plus implementation.
